# Alpha.13 independent supervised confrontation feasibility

The H1–H5 register and future outcome contract from alpha.12 remain byte-exact.
The new machine contract freezes operational descriptor families, scales,
providers, missingness, primary/exploratory comparisons, controls, effective units
and admissible falsification before future model scores. H5 remains primarily a
dependency/split-validity endpoint rather than a forced error correlation.

The response definitions remain `D_RMSE = RMSE_stress - RMSE_reference`,
`S = 1 - RMSE/RMSE_null` and matched-population `D_skill = S_stress-S_reference`.
Native null fitting uses training only. Zero/undefined denominators are unavailable;
unmatched evaluation IDs cannot create a pure structural effect. Target units
remain separate. Contradictory observations cannot be filtered out by sign.

**Zero independent confirmation studies, zero admitted supervised tasks and zero
new real supervised fits.** EcoSIS/CA2023, Perten and litter remain discovery cases.
New targets, folds or estimators cannot reset their roles. The two new material
families are candidates for later source-closed validation, not confirmation units.

Concrete current blockers:

- Tablets have exact original observation/spectrum correspondence, but no verified
  batch/assay dependency ownership or fully admitted original reference task and
  outer/inner split identities. Reference fields remain unopened.
- Soil documents 36 plots but lacks the original-to-canonical plot/reference
  map. A complete object decoder is not a target-free projection, and sequential
  triples cannot establish physical components.
- Cartilage retains 869 verified locations and five joints, with unresolved
  donor/assay closure and preserved earlier predictive nonadmission.
- Beer/wheat original identities and references remain unresolved. Their source
  documentation and package license do not supply the missing closure.

The original tablet publisher abstract and university bibliography were checked;
they do not provide the required downloadable dependency keys. Encountering its
published performance summaries after the X/source freezes did not change
admission, source selection or frozen hypotheses. No numeric published score
enters this protocol. No new native response is available to confirm or contradict
H1–H5. Controlled contrary fixtures test retention logic, not the scientific truth
of the hypotheses.

Accordingly, **the optional pilot is unavailable**, with exact blockers in
[feasibility JSON](benchmarks/alpha13/validation-feasibility.json). No p-value,
study-level confidence interval or rejection is produced. Descriptor variation
in two views cannot identify study-level response uncertainty without admitted
references, matched holdouts and independent studies. Native repeated-CV designs
would not increase the number of studies.

The frozen first model anchors remain raw PLS with components 1/2/3 and nested
Ridge with alphas .01/.1/1/10/100; native SNV is a fixed secondary pipeline.
DAG-ML/Methods own grouped inner selection, preprocessing and refit. Exact
whole-component size controls are required; unsuccessful bounded matching does
not establish that no valid subset exists. FastAOM needs separate admission;
TabPFN stays deferred. See [preregistration](ALPHA13_SUPERVISED_PREREGISTRATION.md),
[contract](benchmarks/alpha13/falsification-contract.json), and
[next bounded phase](ALPHA14_CONFRONTATION_PLAN.md).
