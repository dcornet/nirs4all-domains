# Alpha.13 supervised falsification preregistration — v0.1

This is an operational extension of the unchanged alpha.12 H1–H5 register and
future-outcome definitions. It does not admit any actual supervised task. Freeze
exact source/population/target/units, reference ownership, folds, retained IDs,
pipelines and cost before any pilot. Source or power failure yields unavailable
or inconclusive evidence, never a favorable benchmark certificate.

## Locked descriptors

H1 uses the complete prespecified stability family (raw/SNV × .6/.8 × both
HDBSCAN settings), including ARI median/p10, noise and pair support. Alpha.12
and the calibrated alpha.13 design have separate descriptor versions; missing
support is not zero separation. The family cannot be narrowed after seeing Y.

H2 primary is the raw full-axis fraction at radius .01 × sqrt(full trace),
with k=8 neighborhoods, and its explicitly scoped train/evaluation analogue
when a train-fitted reference is admitted. The other registered radii and SNV
are exploratory secondary descriptors, fixed before responses. A full-source
descriptive profile is not substituted for a prospective training reference.

H3 primary family is raw/SNV primary-setting partition ARI and mean k=8 full-axis
neighbor-set overlap on identical IDs. H4 primary family is raw/SNV retained
PCA fraction, first-PC fraction and train-fitted continuous support when admitted;
noise/all-noise does not supply a qualified continuum label. H5 uses numerical
proximity and the independently audited identity tier as separate variables,
then audits how verified dependency closure changes split validity. It is not
forced into a regression on model error or proof of hidden technical repeats.

The machine-readable binding contract pins source hashes and descriptor paths,
missingness, provider, population, response and controls. Threshold/representation/
k/radius/model choice cannot depend on future outcomes. No composite risk score.

## Discovery, confirmation and study accounting

EcoSIS/CA2023, Perten and litter remain exploratory, including future new models
or splits. Related preparations, targets and revisions stay in one original
study/verified population unit. Unknown potential overlap cannot cross discovery
and confirmation roles; resolve it or group conservatively. A package citation
or a different filename does not establish an independent original experiment.

New UCPH pharmaceutical tablets, original Abisko soil and original beer are
candidate source populations, not automatically admitted confirmation studies.
Their independent reference/specimen ownership and exact local/source content
correspondence must be audited before promotion. The two soil outcomes from the
same 108 samples are one population, not two studies; 36 soil plots or repeated
folds are not 36 studies. Mirrors of the same study share a role.

Freeze source-based study/population roles and sampling biases before numeric
confirmatory outcomes. Any future eligibility change is a new registered contract.
No original study used for tuning/interpretation can regain untouched status.

## Outcomes, anchors and controls

For the same target definition/unit and matched evaluation IDs:
`D_RMSE = RMSE_stress - RMSE_reference`;
`S = 1 - RMSE/RMSE_null`, with each null fitted on its training outcomes only;
`D_skill = S_stress - S_reference`. Zero/undefined null makes skill unavailable.
An estimator interaction is `D_RMSE_PLS - D_RMSE_Ridge` on the same IDs/target.
Unmatched evaluation populations get separate descriptive RMSEs, not a pure
structural treatment contrast. No causal shortcut fraction is identified.

Anchor grids, conditional on admitted training support: native PLS components
1,2,3; native Ridge alphas .01,.1,1,10,100. Raw is the primary pipeline; a fixed
native SNV variant is a prespecified secondary contrast. No HPO beyond those grids.
Centering, preprocessing, selection and refit occur inside native DAG-ML training
scopes. FastAOM requires a separate budget/admission; TabPFN remains deferred.

Exact inner/outer component-safe folds and source/reference closures are mandatory
before fitting. Size matching uses existing whole-component matching, never
rowwise thinning of a known repeated group. Report training size, independent
group counts, changed composition, target-range extrapolation, spectral support,
assay/reference dependencies, model/pipeline and original study relationships.
Y_test cannot choose descriptors, splits, models, transforms or stopping rules.
X-aware population splitting is explicitly transductive, not prospective.

## Analysis, multiplicity and stopping

A bounded pilot is feasibility/exploratory evidence unless a separate confirmatory
power gate is satisfied. Admit at most one new study and the two simple anchors
in the first pilot, after exact task/split preregistration. All scheduled folds,
failures and contrary outcomes remain in their original denominator; no redraw
or estimator/source substitution based on performance. Stop only for registered
admission, numerical or resource failures, not a desired effect sign.

Confirmatory analysis separates original studies rather than rows, folds or Y
targets. H1–H4 families retain all locked components; H5 has its distinct validity
endpoint. A later statistical plan must define independent-study sample size,
descriptor variation, matched holdout support, effect/noise assumptions, missingness
and study-level uncertainty before claiming power. Holm correction across the
four primary H1–H4 families is planned only if valid native study-level tests and
sufficient independent studies are qualified; no p-values are computed here.
Within-study intervals cannot stand in for new-study uncertainty. No adequate
study-level power is currently claimed from candidate source names alone.

## Falsification and contrary evidence

Retain stable-X/good-transfer and unstable-X/poor-transfer cases, high-overlap/no-
advantage and weak-overlap/good-transfer cases, representation changes without
model-response interaction, and support shifts without deterioration. Verified
independent objects that remain spectrally close challenge the interpretation of
proximity as replication. Missing closure makes H5 untestable, not disproved.

Each is a potential counterexample under the defined scope, not automatic rejection
of a probabilistic study-level hypothesis from one task. A hypothesis can be
contradicted only under its qualified analysis/support; otherwise preserve
inconclusive/unavailable states. No explanatory filtering or threshold retuning.
