executes on **37**, including sources without meaningful regression outcomes or
verified biological identities. Five unavailable entries retain access, rights
and dimension reasons. No matrix is downsampled to satisfy a bound.

X0 uses native Methods FlexiblePCA, full trace, optional exact full moments,
nearest neighbors and two fixed HDBSCAN settings. All source observations and

independence. Full-population X0 is descriptive; it is not a train-reference
model for prospective validation. X1 separately uses train-fitted canonical
coverage. HDBSCAN held-out assignment remains unavailable.

Primary HDBSCAN returns multiple non-noise groups in 36 of 37 profiles and no
groups in one. **These are descriptive parameter-specific partitions, not 36
stable separated populations.** Their parameter agreement and membership/noise
support are reported; broad resampling stability is unqualified. Continuous
gradients and unknown physical/acquisition mechanisms remain alternatives.
The previous CA2023 all-noise result used development physical means and its
frozen PCA/settings; the broad full-acquisition/full-population profile has a
different representation and cannot overturn it.

Original-space neighbors are computationally supported in **12 of 37** X0
profiles. None has a neighbor within the largest prespecified numerical
tolerance, 1e-6 times sqrt(full trace). This is not evidence that the remaining
25 sources lack redundancy, nor a biological repeatability result. PCA-space
neighbors remain available separately and can conceal omitted-axis differences.
No physical samples are merged because of distance. Trace/distances retain
source-amplitude units; no cross-source trace or raw-RMSE ranking is produced.

## A second independently sourced descriptive case

Brookhaven crops has 183 finite original-keyed nitrogen pairs. X-partition eta²
is .0795 and species eta² .0607 under the stated observation-level description.
Weak observed species association differs from litter; unknown plant/assay
ownership still prevents model admission and a low-dependence classification.
Cartilage now has X-only geometry and joint/bone-type agreement on 2605 scans;
the five joints remain distinct from independent donors. No Cartilage predictive
model is fitted and no population is reduced below a software threshold.

The cohort is dominated by EcoSIS and includes shared preparations and revisions.
The 37/42 availability and 12/37 raw-neighbor denominators describe this practical
cohort, not prevalence across NIRS applications or independent studies. Findings
support estimator/pipeline-dependent interpretation, while missing source and
assay identities prevent a general low-dependence conclusion.
