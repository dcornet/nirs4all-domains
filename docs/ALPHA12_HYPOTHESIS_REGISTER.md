# Unsupervised hypotheses for later supervised validation — v0.1

Frozen before alpha.12 real-data unsupervised execution. No supervised response
is calculated in alpha.12. These are hypotheses, including their possible
falsification, not conclusions about shortcut learning or probabilities of risk.

| Hypothesis | Locked X descriptor | Later response | Potential falsification |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1: reproducible separation predicts structural-holdout fragility | Per-representation/fraction/parameter ARI, noise and co-membership support; reference group support | Matched evaluation-unit RMSE difference and null-relative skill deterioration under admissible structural versus reference splits | Reproducibly separated populations transfer well after controlling training size/range; unstable groups transfer poorly |

Do not tune these descriptors, radius grid or operational stability gates using
historical or future RMSE. Retain contrary cases. No composite risk score and no
assumption that a successful predictor identifies a causal shortcut mechanism.

## Discovery and untouched confirmation

CA2023/EcoSIS, Perten and litter have already informed model interpretation.
Their supervised responses are exploratory for hypothesis construction, even
when a new split or estimator is later run. They cannot supply untouched
confirmation. Related preparations/revisions/targets remain in the same study
unit when correspondence is supported; unknown overlap prevents independence
claims and must be resolved or conservatively grouped before confirmation.

Before observing new supervised responses, freeze original-study or verified
physical-population discovery/confirmation membership, estimator/pipeline grids,
all target definitions, units, folds, size/range-matched comparisons and exclusions.
Do not split catalog entries from one study across those roles. The present
bank does not yet establish sufficient independent studies or biological groups
for a calibrated power calculation; confirmatory sample size remains pending.

Control training sample/component count, target-range extrapolation, group support,
study/source relationships, model family and preprocessing. Define one paired
response per task/design; repeated fitted designs quantify sensitivity rather
than independent study replication. Keep RMSE in its target units; compare
null-relative skill only under a fixed denominator and report zero/undefined
denominators. Confidence over new studies needs study-level support, separately
from conditional OOF/component intervals. Any later revision is a new registered
protocol and cannot silently replace this one.

No TabPFN installation, weights acquisition, model optimization, target
intervention or main supervised validation is authorized by this register.
