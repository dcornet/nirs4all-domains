# Proposed bounded alpha.14: independently falsifiable confrontation

First resolve original specimen, batch, plot and assay ownership for tablets and
soil, together with selective original field IO and exact source/reference keys.
Verify independent original studies and conservatively group unresolved overlap.
No additional source view, target, fold or repeated fit becomes another study.
Historical discovery cases stay exploratory. Obtain a reviewed storage/compute
budget before acquisition or numerical execution; current ceilings are not a
standing authorization for a later release increase.

Before any new response, freeze exact dataset/content/source/target/units,
study roles, indivisible components, outer and inner fold identities, descriptors,
reference/stress/size controls, matched evaluation IDs and stopping rules. Keep
the locked H1–H5 families and preserve contrary/null/unavailable observations.
Study-level power must use a meaningful scientific contrast and qualified native
uncertainty infrastructure; do not choose an arbitrary minimum study count or
effect threshold from these X-only results.

If one new study meets its gates, execute at most one explicitly exploratory
pilot with the frozen PLS and nested Ridge anchors. An initial sizing bound is
three frozen designs × five outer folds × (three inner folds × eight candidates
+ two selected refits) = 390 native model fits for the raw primary pair. This is
a prospective cost bound, not automatic admission or confirmation. Any fixed SNV
secondary needs its own reviewed additional cost. No bank-wide HPO or TabPFN.

Compare responses on the same target and matched IDs. Record training size,
component counts, X support, target extrapolation and changed composition.
H5 tests actual dependency closure and split validity separately from H1–H4's
predictive relationships. No causal shortcut fraction or universal score.

Only after independent validation should structural evidence select benchmarks
for new estimator comparisons. If ownership or power remains inadequate, publish
the exact unavailable/inconclusive result and continue source closure rather
than weaken thresholds or reuse discovery studies as confirmation.
