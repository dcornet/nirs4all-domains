# Alpha.8 proposed bounded multi-dataset qualification

Design only; no alpha.8 campaign is executed in alpha.7. Test transfer of the
qualified diagnostics beyond EcoSIS rather than selecting datasets for an
attractive shortcut conclusion. Keep the same numerical ownership and separate
association, prediction, split sensitivity and effective intervention evidence.

## Cases and admission, fixed before model results

Use at most three scientific cases: EcoSIS as the frozen reference, one legally
accessible non-leaf composition/regression dataset with documented independent
samples and explanatory batch/site/specimen metadata, and one documented
multi-instrument or repeated-preparation dataset. Shootout is a candidate for
the latter only after original physical/reference alignment and genuine
instrument metadata are verified. Its fragile X clusters do not supply those
identities. CORN/UCPH remain candidates requiring the prerequisites already
recorded in REAL_DATA_PILOT.md, not claimed ready data sources.

Qualify access/licence, catalog/content version, source hashes and target units
through Datasets before acquisition. Require explicit task source/target/split,
one admissible physical/reference outcome per vote, and all indivisible links.
At least 30 declared components, two supported factor levels and enough whole
components for the same inner-CV/intervention gates are desirable admission
conditions. If a factor is unavailable, retain pure-X and suitable descriptive
evidence without inventing replacement labels. Freeze admission from metadata
and scientific contrast before inspecting predictive outcomes. Two EcoSIS
preparations of the same CA2023 plants do not count as independent datasets.

## Bounded schedule per newly admitted task

Freeze one seed-17 reference design, one shuffled-component design, one true
metadata-factor holdout where closure permits it, and one primary factor.
Use the same explicit PLS grid and preprocessing where dimensions/units permit;
document a smaller grid from technical rank/sample prerequisites before runs.
Use three size-control orders on matched evaluation intersections, original-Y
post-evaluation range diagnostics, and at most 19 global / 19 within-factor
training interventions on the reference design. Audit every inner scope before
execution with alpha.7's six-swappable-component and 50% movable/changed-target
thresholds. Record unsuccessful or low-power attempts without redraw. A single
additional seed 31 may be a prespecified sensitivity if the first task's resource
budget allows it, never a search for the best split.

## Qualification and interpretation

Retain per-case native lineage, target maps, source/factor provenance, matched
IDs and 500-draw component intervals conditional on frozen OOF predictions.
Report target units and own-null gains per task; do not pool raw RMSE across
different targets or call a ratio a universal risk score. Compare qualitative
patterns: supported group baselines, genuine unseen-level transfer, size/range
alternatives, effective within-factor loss and explicit non-identifiability.
With only three cases, do not infer population-wide diagnostic accuracy or a
cross-dataset significance result.

Before any operational recommendation, preserve held-out labels, rerun the
leakage adversaries on every new identity pattern, test deterministic replay,
and obtain actual Windows/Ubuntu native CI. A broader methods/representation
search, causal attribution and frontend remain outside this bounded campaign.
