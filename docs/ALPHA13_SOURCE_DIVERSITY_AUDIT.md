# Alpha.13 source diversity and rights audit

Soil has an axis-provenance limitation: OHPL documentation describes 400–2500 nm,
while the 700 canonical labels span 1100–2500 nm. Original-axis correspondence
is unverified. These calculations preserve the complete canonical grid without
relabeling/interpolation; original wavelength-specific conclusions are unqualified.

All **164 pinned entries** are reviewed through catalog descriptors: 42 public
and 122 private. The inventory distinguishes four rights dimensions and reviews
original sources instead of equating private tier with prohibition or public
availability with unrestricted rights. No bank-wide download occurred.

Two additional nonvegetation X-only views are admitted: pharmaceutical tablets
and Abisko soil. UCPH's [original dataset index](https://ucphchemometrics.com/datasets/)
permits public use of its downloadable material. Exact cached statement hashes,
source pages and the OHPL soil package lineage are recorded in the admission.
Access, local analysis and attributed nonreconstructive statistics are admitted;
raw redistribution remains unknown and is not requested. No raw data are shipped.

| View | Original-study lead | Current identity evidence |
|---|---|---|
| UCPH tablets, 310 × 404 | Dyrby et al. 2002, Applied Spectroscopy 56(5), 579–585; DOI [10.1366/0003702021955358](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1366/0003702021955358) | Every canonical spectrum/axis matches the original flat source through explicit observation/source keys; physical, batch and assay closure still unverified. |
| OHPL soil, 108 × 700 | Rinnan and Rinnan 2007, Soil Biology & Biochemistry 39, 1664–1673; original UCPH Abisko collection | 36 plots and three subsamples per plot are documented; exact original-to-canonical plot/reference keys remain unresolved. No triplet assignment by row position. |

These are two additional original-study **candidates**, not two admitted
confirmation studies. The original soil and OHPL package view describe one
collection. Package-paper citations, targets and spectral revisions are not
independent studies. OSSL MIR/VIS-NIR distinctions and unknown overlaps remain
explicit. Historical related vegetation views still dominate the bank.

Datasets' public native acquisition retrieves original ZIPs of 3,822,359 and
431,935 bytes. It also expands nested members: the measured cache is 8,536,787
bytes. A conservative additional 8 MiB reservation covers that observed expansion
inside the unchanged campaign envelope. The initial soil header pass failed on a
MacOS resource fork; its receipt is preserved and the metadata pass excludes
those platform files. No target cells were decoded.

The original tablet MATLAB Matrix combines spectral and nonspectral columns.
Automatic review rejected a complete soil-object load because it could decode
reference fields. That action was not retried. Authorized canonical X-only CSVs
provide the safe numerical inputs. A separately declared additional tablet
audit uses the documented original flat cache with `usecols`: only source keys
and 404 bands are materialized, then joined through declared keys. All 310 rows
match exactly. Reference, type and scale fields are excluded.

The publisher abstract confirms the pharmaceutical study but does not supply
per-observation assay/dependency keys. Detailed methods were unavailable through
the public route checked. Published model summaries were encountered only after
source/design freezes; they did not select sources, thresholds or outcomes and
their numeric scores were not imported into any protocol. This does not create
an untouched confirmation claim.

Beer and wheat remain documented candidates without new spectral acquisition or
model scores. CORN retains the owner's earlier pending-rights decision. No
upstream repository or remote issue was modified.
