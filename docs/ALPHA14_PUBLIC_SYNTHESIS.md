# Public synthesis and confidential scientific evidence

Alpha.14 provides one scientific pipeline with three evidence populations:

- The public scientific corpus retains all historical published results.
- The authorized confidential corpus retains source-scoped local findings and
  reproducibility evidence under its own rights and access controls.
- Public synthesis contains only separately cleared, disclosure-reviewed exports.

No new private numerical synthesis or private source participation is approved
for automatic release. Local analysis authorization does not imply publication
authorization. Detailed results and original-to-pseudonymous mappings therefore
stay outside Git. A confidential scientific result can be useful without creating
any public numeric artifact.

The public alpha.13 corpus remains available unchanged: 41 raw/SNV views under
their documented original scopes, with no inference of 41 independent studies.
Its 6 robust / 17 fragile / 1 insufficient revised real settings remain exactly
those historical source/setting configurations. They are not combined with hidden
private results to produce an undocumented denominator or shortcut prevalence.

Internal comparisons retain full-source PCA concentration, neighborhoods,
multi-scale proximity, raw/SNV agreement, parameter-specific partitions and
supported B60 evidence. Source normalization, material/modality, observation
support and unresolved study overlap constrain every comparison. Raw distances
and target-unit errors are not pooled across unrelated sources.

H1–H5 and the future response definitions are unchanged. No X-only clustering
finding establishes shortcut learning, a causal shortcut fraction or a safety
certificate. The initial alpha.14 budget excludes a supervised pilot. Target
and reference access, original dependencies, frozen tasks and independent study
support remain separate gates.

To release a later numerical synthesis, define the exact permitted aggregate,
source rights, participation scope, small-cell/complementary controls and all
earlier releases to be considered jointly. Review the candidate in the protected
root, approve its exact bytes, then run outgoing validation. Publishing a renamed
private report is never an accepted substitute for this operation.
