screening record. Screening, lawful local admission, executable source views,

The local campaign uses unchanged native raw/SNV PCA, moment availability,
full-axis or prespecified-query neighborhoods, multi-scale proximity, parameter

labels, panels, original identities and fingerprints stay local. No new private
numerical export is approved by this release. The frozen roster retains source
failures, including invalid axes or nonfinite complete matrices, without row

## D — Study independence

Source views, package citations and different targets do not supply independent
populations. Unknown source overlap and physical/reference ownership remain
explicit. Observation-conditional resampling is not biological uncertainty.
Prior CORN decisions and all historical discovery roles remain in force.

## H — Storage and security

The owner approved ceilings of 15,484,290,753 local combined bytes and 805,306,368
cumulative reservation bytes, retaining 256 MiB/task, 4 MiB evidence/task, one
scientific task at a time and 2 GiB free per volume. New reservations remain bounded
to 256 MiB; exact private usage stays local. No historical deletion or migration.
Shared atomic-metadata accounting corrections and operational mistakes are retained
in [execution notes](ALPHA14_EXECUTION_NOTES.md). Storage guards are cooperative;
no measured RAM cap is claimed.

Closed-schema exports require source publication rights, participation clearance,
exact candidate approval and review of earlier releases jointly. Small-cell and
complementary suppression are configurable safeguards, not anonymity or differential
privacy. Git index blobs, source bytes, packages and synthetic adversaries are
reviewed; private evidence is never automatically uploaded.
