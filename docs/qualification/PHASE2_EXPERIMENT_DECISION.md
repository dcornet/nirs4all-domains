| Criterion | A: train-fitted support / predictive contrast | B: structural robustness |

| Potential new conclusion | A scoped positive, null or contrary case about support versus outcome for a declared estimator/population. Exact canonical source/version/view/hash, target/unit, X/Y mapping, split/reference policy, groups and frozen estimator parameters still require a task freeze. | Requires compatible paired representations, fixed density settings, admissible resampling units and exact original identities. | Stop | Useful baseline unavailable, identity/task mismatch, unapproved parameter change, insufficient source terms, unattainable matched training population or exceeded work/resource bound. Preserve the adverse/unavailable case; no favorable replacement/retry. | No admissible units/compatible comparison, ambiguous population correspondence, excessive work or exhausted bounds. Preserve failures; do not tune parameters/draws to recover a desired partition. |

A is preferred because it targets a missing empirical interpretation with fewer
kernel calls, whereas B should first reuse the extensive saved structural
comparisons. This is a scientific priority, not a guarantee of a favorable
result. Small synthetic tests and the independent human pilot can expose many
software/interpretation problems without spending the real-data reservation.
