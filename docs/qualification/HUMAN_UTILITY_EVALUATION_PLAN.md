# Independent warning-utility evaluation: protocol and blank forms

Status: **prepared for approval; no recruitment, contact, collection or human
judgment**. This is a new prospective utility evaluation, not a replacement for
D5's 18/20 automated expectations, D6's 38 contracts or D6.1's non-blinded AI
review. Their files, responses, assignments and conclusions remain unchanged.

## Question and material admission

Do deployment-aware warnings help a competent NIRS analyst choose a justified
validation check or justified abstention, without unsupported prescription?
Rivals: no useful difference from the reference report; increased clarity with
no decision improvement; or confident but scientifically unjustified advice.
Do not assess agreement with developer-registered answers as ground truth.

Two chemometrics/NIRS reviewers, independent of implementation and qualification
authorship, each review **20 paired cases**, subject to admission. Record their
experience, conflicts and previous exposure before distribution; the two slots
remain unassigned. This yields 40 reviewer-case pairs and 80 variant assessments,
not 40/80 studies. No eligible reviewer is currently claimed.

Use the complete 20 original D5 scenario IDs, selected by their frozen membership,
not their outcomes, from D6's existing 24-pair material. Include negative,
contrary, all-noise, missing-context, MIR/unavailable and synthetic cases; do
not remove the two historical manure discrepancies. Original D5 contains four
explicit artificial scientific fixtures; simulated purposes are not real user
requests. Preserve original source/view/provenance grouping: eight dataset IDs
are not eight certified independent studies. No additional target/view votes.

The original participant v2 archive remains intact and hash-verified (24 pairs,
48 variants, all responses blank). It predates the current presentation review:
using it unchanged assesses those frozen variants only, not current D6.1. For
the proposed current-product evaluation, freeze a new read-only packet of the
same 20 admitted scenarios: current default consumer versus its optional D6.1
decision summary, both at the same approved Git snapshot. Reuse existing APIs
and source aggregates; no numerical fit. Verify source/population/metric hashes
are identical across each pair and preserve any difference in carried evidence
as an admission error. A broken export is corrected before recruitment, never
after inspecting responses. Do not relabel the old archive as a new packet.

Before distribution, a coordinator must verify 20 renderable pairs, source terms
for these recipients, no restricted raw/row-level data or sensitive identities,
blank forms, version hashes, and proper expectation/condition separation. These
checks and packet assembly are still pending approval; this public document
contains no new Research cases or private condition mapping. If fewer than 20
are admissible, report the count and seek a prespecified smaller pilot before
collection; do not replace unavailable cases with favorable outcomes.

## Procedure and partial blinding

1. Give only permitted aggregate observations, exact prediction objective,
   population/representation/method context and limitations. Withhold product
   conclusions, expected checks, old qualification verdicts and the other
   reviewer. Reviewer independently records observation, justified next action,
   prerequisites and unsupported claims (Stage 1); timestamp/lock it.
2. Present two neutral variant labels with identical evidence. Each reviewer
   judges each independently before making a paired comparison (Stage 2).
   Counterbalance ten reference-first/ten decision-first; reverse each pair's
   condition order for the other reviewer. Freeze a source/purpose-separated
   schedule before responses, with no search for favorable ratings.
3. After both variant records are locked, ask which helps the declared decision,
   what changed, what remains unjustified and why. Equivalent/no benefit and
   justified abstention are valid findings. Record learning/recognition effects.
4. Retain all original independent records, blanks and timestamps. Only after
   lock may condition mapping or expectations be revealed for discussion.
   Scientific provenance/readiness fields can reveal condition; collect the
   guessed condition and explanation. Blinding is partial, not certified.

Participants should not read public qualification verdicts or coordinator
materials before lock; prior familiarity is disclosed rather than hidden.
Do not suppress provenance needed for scientific judgment just to claim blinding.

## Prespecified pilot criteria and analysis

These are owner-reviewable convenience gates, not validated scientific
cutoffs, statistical power or population accuracy. Freeze them before collection:

- Feasibility: both reviewers complete at least 16/20 paired cases; report all
  missingness. Otherwise the pilot cannot establish its progression criteria.
- Safety: no confirmed harmful prescription and no unresolved serious overclaim
  can support a positive progression decision. Examples include inventing units,
  mandatory LOGO from a categorical factor, causal claims from clusters, or
  claiming a future-support/verification result that was never observed.
- Fidelity: at least 90% of complete current-variant cases per reviewer judged
  faithful or partial with the missing detail explicitly identified; report
  faithful separately, not only the combined count.
- Decision utility: at least 80% of complete cases per reviewer have a justified
  check or justified abstention; no more than two complete cases per reviewer
  judged less useful than reference. This is an adequacy signal, not proof of
  superiority. Report improved/equivalent/worse/neither/unclear separately.
- Recognizing insufficient evidence is evaluated explicitly on the predeclared
  missing/unavailable/refusal stratum. Any unsupported strong claim in that
  stratum triggers revision, not a favorable average hiding it.

Failure or disagreement is retained, not explained away to reach a target.
Report denominators and paired direction tables **by reviewer and dimension**,
then by documented source/scenario type. Cases/views from one source remain
dependent; do not pool 40 opinions as 40 independent studies or give a binomial
confidence interval for general user efficacy. No hypothesis test, kappa or
composite score is necessary for this small pilot. Raw agreement/disagreement
tables and quoted reasoning are primary. Later effectiveness confirmation needs
more independent analysts/tasks and a separate design.

For disagreement, preserve both source-based positions. A third independent
adjudicator can be requested only with owner approval after locked responses;
do not invent consensus or erase the minority. Unresolved serious harm keeps
the progression decision pending. Developer expectations are not an answer key.
