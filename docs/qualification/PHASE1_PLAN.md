## Admission and resource bounds

Phase 1 permits source/report review, replay of existing evidence, modest
synthetic software tests, public deployment checks, targeted reversible fixes
and prospective designs. No new real-source analysis, fitted predictor,
research campaign, D7/Alpha, upstream modification, release, tag or version
change is admitted. Research is read-only, including its current indexes and
all original reports, failures, journals and reservations.

Reuse the completed Windows/Ubuntu scientific/browser/report gates, four
native/WASM fixtures and public smoke. Additional local storage is bounded to
16 MiB, persistent receipts to 4 MiB, with no new campaign reservation or ceiling
increase and at least 2 GiB free per volume. One serial synthetic timing probe
may reuse the existing 32-by-2 separated fixture: one complete Domains analysis
and one direct-provider series, at most two PCA, three nearest-neighbor and two
HDBSCAN calls in total. It has a 960 MiB process-tree commit/RSS bound, 600-second
wall bound and 64 KiB result bound. No repeats, tuning, real data or scaling
claim follows from one timing observation. CI handles necessary native builds;
no local native/toolchain rebuild is budgeted.

## Expected behavior fixed before corrective tests

1. Preserve the frozen browser plan. Enforce its absolute `1e-10` analytical
   scalar expectation in the common native/WASM checker and fixture assertion;
   keep inter-backend absolute/relative `1e-8` unchanged. Existing direct WASM
   eigenvalue-1/eigenvalue-4 checks at `1e-12` stay intact. An error of `5e-10`
   near one must pass the platform comparison but fail the independent
   analytical check. No historical output is replaced or reclassified as false.
2. Validate the nearest-neighbor response contract before reading its first
   slot: admissible distinct references in nondecreasing finite distance order,
   followed only by absent-neighbor `(-1, null)` padding. Valid equal distances,
   complete rows and trailing padding remain admissible. A decreasing row or
   padding followed by a valid reference must yield `error`, reason
   `INVALID_PROVIDER_RESPONSE`, and null value, without reordering, filling or
   recomputing neighbors. Self/equal-group exclusions retain their exact checks.
   The discovered gap is demonstrated with adversarial provider responses;
   it is not evidence that the pinned Methods backend emitted an incorrect row.
3. Correct documentary claims that the controller/exporter is absent or
   deployment is pending; bind the replacement claims to actual commits/runs.
   Keep saved/native/browser scope differences and original-language scientific
   prose explicit. No interpretation threshold or numerical algorithm changes.

Qualification has separate levels: implementation availability, tested numerical
behavior on stated fixtures, source/scope validity, and practical external
utility. Passing one level does not imply the next. Shared Methods native/WASM
parity is not an independent algorithm oracle; within-fit persistence is not
resampling stability; views, targets and simulated intents are not independent
studies. Unavailable and contrary evidence must remain visible.

Additional source-review finding, fixed before its regression test: plain
dataset/finding text in Markdown must not become an active image/link or alter
the surrounding structure. Escape Markdown delimiters outside the fenced
original JSON, while preserving the semantic report and numerical evidence.
The current exporter escapes HTML and fences evidence but permits image/link
syntax in ordinary text. This is a downstream Markdown-renderer concern, not
an observed upload by the application or an HTML CSP failure.

## Completion and deferred decisions

Deliver the software anomaly report, eleven-capability evidence matrix, critical
D1–D6.1 review, methodological gaps, prioritized UX/software roadmap, draft
train-only support protocol, ranked candidate experiments with bounded resource
estimates, and an owner decision summary. Every claim must identify source and
scope; machine receipts identify actual executions versus source inspection.

The train-only protocol is a proposal for owner review, not an implemented
browser feature or an authorization to fit. Phase 2 requires an explicit owner
decision on a content-bound eligible cohort, hypotheses, units, unchanged
methods, negative controls, endpoints, numerical tolerances, rights and budgets.
