# Prospective robustness design, not an executed campaign

Reuse R1/R2, P0/P1/P2 and R3d source-bound evidence first. Cartilage already
discriminates high neighborhood overlap from low partition agreement; stable,
all-noise, uninformative and unavailable cases are controls, not exclusions.
This proposal addresses a distinct missing question: how much of a descriptor
changes with admissible representations, fixed density settings and sampling
units? It does not amend historical B60 thresholds or H1–H5.

## Fixed comparisons and adversaries

Select at most two eligible original-study families by metadata/rights/size
before quantitative outcome review. Retain full original sources/axes/IDs;
representations and resampling membership are explicit views, not source edits.
Use raw and the existing Methods SNV with no parameter search. Additional
smoothing/derivative/MSC families require separately frozen owner parameters;
their historical evidence remains available without a new fit.

First compare full-source raw/SNV under fixed HDBSCAN EOM settings 8/4 and 12/4,
allow_single_cluster=false. Keep PCA rule, original populations and query/reference
definitions compatible. Do not select the setting that preserves a desired
cluster count. Separate parameter contrasts within a representation from paired
representation contrasts at the same settings.

For a later resampling component, freeze 60 whole-unit subsamples per source
with the existing native sampler, before execution. The exact eligible-unit
roster, fraction and seed list must be part of the task freeze. Preserve all
acquisitions/reference-connected units together. If physical units are unknown,
an observation-resampling sensitivity result must be labeled potentially
optimistic; it cannot estimate physical-unit robustness. No new groups or
crossed-factor hierarchy are fabricated. A technically unattainable draw is
recorded unavailable rather than retried until favorable.

PCA stability uses train/subsample-fitted state and sign/tied-space-invariant
score/projector geometry, resolved rank and full-trace coverage, with exact
moment availability recorded separately. Neighborhood comparisons record
intersection/k, query coverage, exclusions and reference membership; a changed
reference pool is not a pure representation effect. Partition ARI/contingency,
cluster sizes, noise and reassignment require exact matched observation IDs.
For unequal subsamples, restrict partition agreement to the declared common
observations and disclose coverage; do not invent out-of-sample HDBSCAN labels.
Within-fit persistence remains separate from both parameter and resampling
variation. Do not present dependent-resample percentiles as independent-study
confidence intervals.

Technical-repeat adversaries distinguish repeated acquisitions from new physical
specimens, retain declared reference dependencies and show observation-weight
effects without calling duplicate support new independent evidence. Crossed
experimental factors are tested with original memberships. Small/unit-poor
populations, constant spectra, unresolved PCA and all-noise/trivial partitions
must retain explicit uninformative/unavailable states rather than favorable
agreement scores. Stable negative controls, continuously structured/no-cluster
cases and adverse representation changes must survive reporting.

## Hypotheses, failure criteria and bounds

Competing explanations are stable geometry/substructure, density-threshold
sensitivity, representation-dependent geometry and repeat/population weighting.
The preregistered output is a paired distribution of descriptor changes with
counts, scopes and failures, not a winner or causal shortcut. No fixed outcome
is required for success; an unavailable effective-unit denominator blocks a
stronger robustness claim. Predictive fragility requires its own useful-baseline
contrast and cannot be inferred from these X-only variations.

Initial estimate: <=2 sources x 2 representations x 60 draws = 240 resampling
PCA fits, 480 HDBSCAN fits and 480 neighbor calls. Including four full-source
anchor PCA fits, eight anchor density fits and eight anchor neighbor calls,
the total caps are 244 PCA, 488 HDBSCAN and 488 neighbor calls. Reuse compatible
saved anchors when available, without increasing these caps. One serial
process, 1 hour, 960 MiB RAM, <=32 MiB local and
<=6 MiB total reservations; no acquisition or raw/fold copies. Admission must
verify actual work bounds/dimensions and fixed draw membership before approval;
otherwise execution remains unavailable. No automatic retries or budget raise.
These are proposed maxima, not measured costs or authorization. This option and
the 6 MiB predictive contrast compete for the same last-measured campaign
headroom; they cannot both be admitted under that remaining envelope. Prefer
the narrower scientifically discriminating question after protocol review.
