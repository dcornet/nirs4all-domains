# Critical evidence review and methodological gaps

This review reuses frozen source-permitted aggregate reports. Restricted raw
matrices, row predictions and uncleared confirmation statistics are not copied.
The source reports below are available in the owner's private Research repository
at [5831f6f](https://github.com/dcornet/nirs4all-structural-research/tree/5831f6f868cbcab7f82763b8334a258cdd35ca85/reports).
They are historical evidence, not rerun experiments or a new study sample.

## Alternative explanations and contrary cases

Cartilage's recorded overlap is mean neighbor intersection divided by k, not
Jaccard. Full-X overlap 0.951171875 and PCA overlap 0.94921875 coexist with
raw/smooth partition ARI 0.17840826957803516. At matched HDBSCAN 8/4 settings,
counts are 2 and 10; at 12/4 they are 6 and 6. This separates geometric
preservation, representation effects and density-threshold sensitivity. It
does not establish stable physical subpopulations, preprocessing superiority,
resampling stability or predictive fragility.

Tablet factor association can be strong in continuous PC1 geometry while
discrete cluster agreement is weak. Factor relevance to a stated deployment
still requires original identities and context. Crossed factors need not form
a hierarchy. Equal reported date-association statistics do not authenticate
identical memberships or provide independent confirmation. Manure's one of 52
observed evaluation spectra beyond a training PC1 range is narrow observed-X
evidence; zero or few PC1 exceedances do not certify multivariate/future support.
Stable/all-noise/uninformative and unavailable cases remain visible rather than
being selected out or converted into safety claims.

## Predictive correspondence is still unresolved

`R3C_RELEASE_REPORT.md` and `R3D_RELEASE_REPORT.md` preserve three untouched
external-feasibility families (two numerically transferable), estimator/track-
dependent gain signs and a study-count gate of eight not reached. Discovery
geography contributed a large share of gain and the meta-training sample was
near rank saturation. R3d replayed stored forecasts under fit-denial guards;
it did not add independent confirmation or fit a calibrated risk predictor.
One source's aggregate-transfer clearance remains missing; its numeric results
stay local. Product Windows/Ubuntu CI does not retroactively qualify the old
Research platform runs, which disclose historical failures and unexecuted CI.

Poor reference skill cannot establish structural fragility. A structural marker
may reflect acquisition context, continuous nuisance variation, density
thresholds, representation or training support; its predictive relevance also
depends on Y and the estimator. Identical X can support different target
relationships. Existing contradictory results must survive any later analysis.

## Gaps that block stronger conclusions

- Unknown independent-study and physical/reference unit denominators; incomplete
  original crosswalks. Retain exploratory scope rather than invent groups.
- Unequal evidence availability and repeated source/view/target cases; no
  defensible prevalence or meta-study p-value from case counts.
- Four common parity fixtures and a limited independent analytical oracle;
  tied eigenspaces, truncation/full-moment checks, near-resolution and difficult
  conditioning still need targeted numerical qualification.
- Within-fit persistence, parameter sensitivity and resampling robustness are
  different. No new resampling claim follows from selected-branch support.
- No browser fitted-state/transform consumer; native availability is not browser
  portability. PC1 range evidence does not replace multivariate support.
- Too few independent confirmation families, weak-baseline/rank concerns,
  estimator dependence and no independent warning-utility assessment.
