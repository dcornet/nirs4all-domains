# Confidential cross-source synthesis — domains.synthesis.v0.1

Expected behavior is fixed before tests and before new native summaries of real
evidence. H1–H5, their binding contract and alpha.12/13 numerical protocols remain
byte-exact. This protocol adds exploratory comparisons of already calculated
descriptors; it does not change their definitions or select them using Y.

Reconcile catalog entries, declared spectral source views and actual execution
receipts by explicit source/content/protocol identities. Audit cards are not
executions. Keep failed, partial, unauthorized and unavailable cases. Identical
source/content profiles from earlier releases are reused once, with all original
receipt references retained. Raw/SNV are paired representations, not new studies.
Source hashes from different populations never join by row position.

Original studies and populations require independently supported identities.
Bibliographic groups and unique row labels do not qualify them. Verified shared
populations/components remain shared; unknown overlap stays unknown. Source-view
summaries remain descriptive and cannot supply study-level standard errors,
p-values, prevalence or an effective independent sample size. Report verified
unit counts separately from the unknown scientific denominator.

The fixed comparison columns are raw/SNV PC1/PC5/retained fractions, optional
exact effective dimension, both fixed HDBSCAN group/noise summaries, normalized
full-axis nearest-distance summaries and dispersion, all seven frozen proximity
radii, raw/SNV primary partition ARI and k=8 neighbor overlap. Preserve exact
complete-source versus sampled-query support, source n/p, metadata availability,
factor missingness/cardinality and verified/declarative semantics. Existing B60
fractions, settings, exposure/eligible ARI support and observation/physical scope
remain separate, with their original protocol identities. No new PCA/B60 campaign.

Compare per-representation descriptor distributions, paired within-source changes,
availability and missingness, source-family contrasts, and associations with n/p.
Native Methods summaries own means, quantiles and sample SD. Native
correlation selector create/fit/output_cols C ABI, with threshold=t and top_k=-1, screens
absolute Pearson association at fixed strict thresholds t=.50/.80/.95. Its
published implementation uses `abs(r) > t`; no coefficient or sign getter is
available. The role facade rejects an empty selection; the published C ABI
correctly returns zero columns and is used directly, with owned/destroyed handles.
Report those exact boolean screens with paired source support, never
invent an exact r, a p-value or a causal relationship. Constant vectors and fewer
than three matched values are unavailable. NumPy is binding transport only.
No coefficient recovery by threshold search or local correlation implementation.

Pairwise-complete descriptor comparisons retain exact participating source IDs
and missingness in protected evidence. Do not pool raw/SNV as independent rows.
Show known population-grouped sensitivity separately where actually identifiable;
unknown overlap prevents an independent-study analysis. Sample-size/spectral-width
relationships and correlated descriptors are exploratory, unadjusted associations,
not proof of redundancy or confounding removal. Do not choose a new H1–H5 subset.

Produce a protected comparative matrix, native summary/association evidence and
standalone figures: descriptor completeness/availability, paired representation
changes, n/p support plots and threshold-screened descriptor relationships.
Source families and modality labels retain their provenance and uncertainty.
Observation-conditional resampling cannot become biological uncertainty.
Report numerical repeatability of reused receipts/hashes separately from new
resampling sensitivity; byte-identical reuse is not a fresh numerical refit.

For prospective supervision, rank by source/reference closure and affordable
component-safe design before any new model scores. Original keyed X/Y equivalence,
target units, assay/physical ownership and frozen splits are separate gates.
Missing closure may permit keyed development-only XY description, never OOF or
independent generalization by relabeling. No retained Y in selection. An X-aware
full-population split is explicitly transductive. The PLS 1/2/3 and Ridge
.01/.1/1/10/100 anchors remain unchanged; any real pilot needs a separate exact
freeze and owner-reviewed fit count/budget. No TabPFN.

Expected adversarial outcomes: cards without receipts do not increase execution
counts; shared views do not increase verified populations; public visibility
does not override analysis rights; publication denial preserves science; missing
assay ownership refuses independent prediction; target mutation cannot change
the X-only matrix; unmatched outcomes remain descriptive; negative/null model
responses are retained; incomplete moments remain unavailable. IDs, receipts and
freeze hashes compare exactly. Native analytical synthetic summaries use absolute
1e-10; threshold fixtures stay away from boundary roundoff. Same-provider repeated
screens and identity/hash replay are exact. No tolerance changes to old protocols.

All real comparative values and figures stay in the protected local environment.
Use the existing alpha.14 exact-byte and cumulative disclosure machinery. A small
candidate review may reject publication; no sanitized scientific substitution,
automatic upload, anonymity claim or new privacy framework is introduced.
