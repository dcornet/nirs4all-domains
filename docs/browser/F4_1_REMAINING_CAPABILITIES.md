# F4.1 remaining capabilities

F4.1 adds component-specific associations with recorded raw-PCA scores. It does
not provide a global spectral association score, causal attribution, leakage
probability, calibration performance or an automatic preprocessing/split choice.
Missing factors cannot establish absence of structure or confounding.

The numerical owner remains Methods 1.3.13 / ABI 2.20; intrinsic qualification
remains Preflight 0.1.6. No upstream repository is changed. Existing browser
PCA/density/support protocols and D1–D6.1 evidence remain distinct.

Only unrestricted exact inference with one acquisition per externally qualified
independent unit is considered, at most eight rows and 40,320 arrangements per
test. The complete prospective factor-by-resolved-PC family uses owner Holm
adjustment. An external record is a supplied assumption, not authenticated by
Domains. Unknown or repeated physical/reference units, missingness, blocking,
post-exploration selection or an incomplete family leave inference unavailable.
The descriptive statistics survive. No custom permutations or aggregation are
substituted. BH was inventoried but is not part of the qualified protocol.

Mixed models, constrained/block/hierarchical permutations, observational
confounder adjustment, nonlinear dependence tests and global spectral-space
estimands require separate contracts and owner capabilities. Comparisons across
preprocessing families require a later prospective multirepresentation protocol;
F4.1 does not tune a favorable representation or rotate PCs using M.

Per-PC signs depend on the recorded basis; tied eigenspaces may rotate. Missing
M uses complete pairs for that factor, without refitting PCA or removing rows
from the imported dataset. Source PCA memory/work limits remain applicable.

Ordinary aggregate tables contain no per-row M vectors or codes. Local factor
colors reveal observation membership or value bins: their export requires a
separate, current content/PCA/factor-bound source-permission declaration.
Selecting all report annexes cannot grant it. This declaration does not create
legal rights. Uncolored PCA coordinates and aggregate evidence remain available.

No new real-data experiments, acquisitions or scientific reservations are part
of this delivery. Research replication is a separate authorized activity.
