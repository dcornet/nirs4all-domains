## Scientific limits

All new execution is synthetic software/numerical qualification, not empirical
validation on real studies. Associations concern recorded component scores and
the admitted observation population. Repeats are acquisition weighted, source
labels are declarations, and physical/reference independence is not certified.
External design authenticity, prospectivity and the chosen measure remain
external assumptions. Missing factors, unqualified inference or a null PC do
not establish absence of structure. Causal mechanisms, leakage probabilities,
calibrability, global spectral effects and preprocessing prescriptions remain
outside this protocol. See [remaining capabilities](F4_1_REMAINING_CAPABILITIES.md).
