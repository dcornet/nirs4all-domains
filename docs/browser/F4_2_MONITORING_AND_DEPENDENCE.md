# F4.2 monitoring and dependence

Methods `statsPcaMonitor` uses reference row index modulo `n_folds`, not grouped
or randomized folds. Its empirical reference values are type-7 quantiles of
out-of-fold T²/Q at `1-alpha`. These describe that indexed-fold construction;
alpha is not a coverage guarantee under dependence, small samples or shift.
The complete provider numerical-domain and rank restrictions remain applicable.

The default qualification is unknown. Repeated acquisitions/specimens or shared
analytical-reference identities provide evidence against row independence.
Domains records within-fold/cross-fold and train/test sharing without inventing
physical units. Campaign/instrument/group correspondence is documentary context;
a categorical column alone does not establish an indivisible experimental unit
or require a particular validation split. An external plan may explicitly name
additional indivisible dependency fields; their declared boundaries are checked.

An optional `domains.monitor_indexed_folds_design.v1` plan must bind dataset
content, ordered train keys, `max_components`, `n_folds`, `alpha`, unique
independent-unit keys, its source and explicit modulo-fold authorization.
Its source is external evidence, not an independence checkbox or certification
issued by Domains. Known contradicting dependencies keep stronger limit claims
unavailable. Missing identity information remains missing. Limits can be
conditionally interpreted only under the supplied external assumptions.

Descriptive T²/Q, owner limits, near-limit states, counts/proportions and centroid
diagnostics may remain visible when inference is unqualified, with that scope
stated beside them. An unqualified limit is never drawn as a qualified confidence
contour. A crossing is not a probability of a bad prediction or shortcut. Raw
neighbors and FlexiblePCA position are separate evidence and can disagree.

`descriptive_fractions` divides each owner beyond-limit count by the exact number
of query acquisitions. Numerator and denominator are retained. This elementary
count normalization is protocol assembly, not a new threshold, an independent-unit
coverage estimate or a probability of failure. Near-limit counts remain the
provider's counts. No per-observation binary flags are reconstructed from floats.
