# Supervised pilot admission

The owner-approved alpha.14 resource proposal covers privacy engineering,
screening and X-only analysis. It explicitly excludes a supervised pilot.
No TabPFN installation, weights, fitting or new supervised model is scheduled.

Rights to local X analysis do not supply missing original physical/reference or
assay ownership. Tablets retain unresolved batch/assay closure; Abisko soil
retains its original plot/reference and axis-correspondence limitations; Cartilage
retains donor/assay uncertainty. CORN stays under its prior owner hold. Alternative
private sources are screened without selecting on model scores, but require
their own original identity, target, task and independent-evaluation proof.

A later pilot needs one exact source/content/target/unit, declared dependencies,
matched evaluation identities, component-safe outer/inner folds, fixed PLS1/2/3
and Ridge .01/.1/1/10/100 anchors, frozen controls and a separately reviewed cost.
Raw is primary; any native SNV secondary needs its own cost. Train-null outcomes
use training Y only; retained Y cannot select descriptors or models.

The frozen alpha.12/13 H1–H5, discovery roles, D_RMSE, relative skill and matched
D_skill remain unchanged. One new-study pilot would be exploratory, with failures
and contrary outcomes retained; it cannot establish cross-study confirmation.
Confidential identities and sample predictions need not become public benchmarks.
