## Bounded investigation

- Reuse the actual protected alpha.15 candidate receipts. Investigate only new
  original-reference links; do not rerun unchanged schema audits or X synthesis.
- Up to three previously investigated candidates and, after a recorded stopping
  decision, at most two alternatives from the already screened catalog.
- At most **6 MiB per candidate**, including all stages and retries under the
  same candidate budget identity; at most 4 MiB essential evidence per task.
- Up to 6 MiB for integrated reporting; retain a 12 MiB failure/retry allowance
  within the total 48 MiB envelope. A retry cannot bypass a candidate's 6 MiB cap.
- Existing documents first. At most 4 MiB total new public source documentation,
  included in the above reservations. No confidential identity, key, path or
  scientific value is sent to an external service. No new spectral archive.
- Selective original metadata/X/reference projection only. A target-access
  freeze must precede any numeric target decoding; retained target values remain
  sealed. Development-only keyed numeric correspondence or native descriptive
  XY analysis is allowed only when its specific identity/unit/scope gates pass.
- No new broad PCA/HDBSCAN/B60 campaign, no per-fold/model/target X copies,
  no real PLS/Ridge/FastAOM fit, and no TabPFN installation or execution.

Preserve serial science, 256 MiB/task, the 4 MiB essential-evidence cap, 2 GiB
free-volume reserve, atomic resumable stages and the existing cumulative ledger.
No historical deletion, rewriting, compaction or migration.

Section 9 of the alpha.16 mission states that prior ceilings are not fresh
alpha.16 spending authorization and requires approval for increases. New
source-resolution execution is covered by the recorded decision. A real pilot requires a later
separate owner approval bound to a qualified exact task/folds/providers, native
