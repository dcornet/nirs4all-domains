# D5: decision consistency of conditional warnings

The standalone consumer shows the declared objective, prediction population,
unit and actual split before findings. Checks for unassessable findings stay
readable with their reasons and prerequisites. `testability=available` is a
referenced-prerequisite flag, not permission or proof of execution. Every new
scientific fit requires a separately qualified and authorized protocol.

This presentation refinement reuses [the API/CLI](STANDALONE_REPORT_API.md).
The v1 view/annotation schemas, hashes and D1/D2/D3 rules are unchanged. Different
objectives require separately validated owner annotations; relabeling a report
does not change intended use or establish evaluation evidence.

Research froze 20 simulated decision scenarios before report production, on D4's
eight canonical IDs/eight X views: seven NIR and one unavailable-numeric MIR
outgroup. The cohort includes within-experiment/future-batch tablets, known
acquisitions/new animals in milk, known/new fruit commodities, raw/smooth
cartilage, observed/unobserved manure X, existing leaf/canopy and litter cases,
and explicit negative fixtures. Original reports, memberships and measured
values stay unchanged. Simulated objectives are not actual user declarations.

Eighteen scenarios meet all frozen expectations. Two manure cases retain a
prerequisite-state discrepancy: D1 flags the existing train-fitted PC1 evidence
available, while the proposed multivariate/deployment verification is deferred.
The initial criteria and both nonconformities remain in the matrix. No rule or
threshold changes to erase the distinction.

Twenty automated saved-evidence reviews are separate from 20 deferred new
analyses. The 17 D1 instances contain 18 check records flagged available and 76
requiring information. Those flags are not completed analyses. The view retains
24 observed, 11 conditional, 8 contradicted and 42 unavailable family assessments:
scenario counts, not risk prevalence or independent studies. Original databases
and certified independent-study counts remain unknown.

Local 71 focused owner tests and 8 Research replay tests pass. They check scientific
consistency, original values, negative cases and readable conditions, not user
effectiveness. The Research release records actual platform/browser receipts
separately. No model, PCA or HDBSCAN was fitted. An initial expanded run passed 17
additional historical D1 tests but imported a native synthetic factor-association
fixture, failing the runner's no-numerical-import guard. The delivered runner
retains that guard and the focused transport scope; historical tests stay intact.

No independent expert or user review occurred. Research specifies blinded paired
scenarios, checking against original evidence, explicit feasibility judgments and
independent adjudication. Until executed, the qualification is scientific/contract
consistency, not demonstrated usefulness, expected predictive degradation, a risk
score or a universal validation recommendation.
