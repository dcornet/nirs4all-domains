# Full-bank screening and admission

The pinned screening population is **164 catalog entries: 42 public and 122
private-tier**. The prior inventory is reused; no catalog recollection or blanket
data download. Detailed source-level screening is local and confidential.

Each record separates original study/provenance, declared modality/axes/dimensions,
provided matrix availability, access, local analysis, aggregate publication, raw
redistribution, sensitive handling, extra restrictions, overlap, physical/reference
closure and source costs. Unknowns stay explicit. Primary-source metadata are
distinct from catalog/wrapper license declarations. No original keys are inferred
from row position. The owner's prior CORN hold is preserved.

Owner authorization for provided local data is retained in a protected receipt.
It does not grant publication. Sources outside that scope, unavailable matrices,
unqualified modalities and complete-source work/cost failures remain excluded or
pending. Private-tier sources are not excluded merely because of their label.

The prospective X-only selection uses fixed material-family priorities and cost
ordering, with reviewed caps. Historical admitted sources are not relabeled as
new independent populations. Complete observations and axes remain present.
Deep-source priorities precede reference-result inspection; inadequate planned
exposure retains an explicit unsupported result without a favorable replacement.

Screening, permission-qualified entries, executable source views, completed
analyses, publication-approved evidence and original independent studies have
different denominators. The confidential report retains their exact values.
This public document does not disclose new private participation or numerical
findings without separate clearance. Neither the catalog nor its accessible
subset is a representative probability sample of NIRS studies.
