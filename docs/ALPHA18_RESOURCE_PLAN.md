2. Missing X: at most 95 source tasks, two PCA requests and four conditional

3. Reference: all 1,351 metadata-eligible tasks, at most three development folds,

4. Deeper stress: at most 24 source/target tasks selected by metadata/identity
   and catalog order before outcomes. Matched evaluation and whole-component
