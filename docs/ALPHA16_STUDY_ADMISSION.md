# Original-reference study admission — alpha.16

`domains.alpha16.original_reference.v0.1` reuses alpha.15 admission and native
confrontation. It resolves the original-file boundary before interpreting a
canonical identity. Generated conversion manifests are locator evidence; they
do not establish physical ownership or experimental independence.

| Tier | Necessary evidence | Insufficient substitute |
|---|---|---|
| X-only description | Authorized, qualified complete X with preserved axes and actual execution receipt | Cache presence and declared dimensions |
| Development-only XY | Original keyed X/Y parity, quantitative target meaning/units, frozen development/retained access and missingness policy | Equal row counts, converter order, numeric target IDs |
| Exploratory prediction | Original study, acquisition/specimen/reference/assay ownership, conservative dependency components and feasible safe designs | Unique observations or geometrical clusters |
| Independent confirmation | Untouched verified study/population separation and prospective independent-study support | Another target, view or fold from a discovery population |

An original locator must identify the declared source and complete path. A
same-basename search cannot establish provenance. The strict manifest adapter
also verifies declared filename and full SHA256; partial or ambiguous checksum
schemes remain unresolved. This is the implemented adapter's content-binding
gate, not a claim that all original scientific sources must distribute SHA256.
An independently documented original-source binding can support another narrow
adapter after prospective qualification.

Required keyed fields must exist on both sides and contain values. Two absent
fields are never equivalent. Joins preserve complete identity sets, units and
axes; no problematic rows are removed to pass admission. Numerical transport
parity and physical/reference ownership remain separate results.

Existing CORN restrictions remain in force. EcoSIS/CA2023, Perten and litter
remain discovery populations. Unknown donor, assay, batch or study overlap is
reported as unknown; it is not replaced with independent per-row identities.
Source-specific evidence and decisions are protected, including participation.
Publication permission does not decide local scientific eligibility.

Proposed upstream work, only when a concrete source requires it: selective
structured-field projection belongs in IO/Formats, and partition-aware target
access belongs in Datasets. Domains records the missing public reader/access
contract; it does not implement a replacement numerical backend or alter
upstream repositories.
